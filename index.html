Tuhin Ahmed

Journalist | Community Leader

Tuhin Ahmed is a Bangladeshi journalist and reporter based in Sylhet, serving as a correspondent for Daily Protidiner Sangbad and Bangladesh Guardian. As an Executive Member of the Sylhet Zilla Press Club, he focuses his reporting on local governance, community affairs, regional news, and environmental stewardship across the region.

Alongside his media background, Tuhin actively leads civic and social welfare initiatives. He serves as Assistant Coordinator (Admin) for Rotary District 3282 and was President of the Rotary Club of Sylhet North for the 2025–2026 term, guiding environmental preservation drives, educational distributions, and community outreach campaigns.

Location: Sylhet, Bangladesh Email: contact@tuhin.com.bd
